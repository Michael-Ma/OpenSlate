## Open issues

These are deliberate audit outcomes, not undisclosed failing checks or inferred permission requests.

1. **Confirmed native proof boundary:** native `start()` rechecks config/account/reference bytes after the application dispatch marker but before RPC `turn/start`. An existing fake test proves zero turn starts for changed references, yet an arbitrary post-marker exception cannot safely prove non-dispatch to the server. Options: retain conservative unknown/Give up; add a versioned exact worker refusal proof; redesign the marker callback boundary. Recommend exact worker refusal proof, reviewed with the lead across receipt/transport contracts. Do not turn arbitrary post-marker errors into free retry authority.
2. **Confirmed category limitation; historical causes unproven:** process exit, transport failure, cleanup failure and busy/closed worker stages can still report safe `native_unknown`. Options: keep category-only data; add an allowlisted stage/reason enum; keep bounded stage/exit telemetry privately. Recommend a small lead-owned reason enum while keeping unexplained causes unknown. The snapshot's eight original reasons cannot be reconstructed or relabeled from these new fake reproductions.
3. **Confirmed completed-output recovery gap:** permanently damaged owned output or an expired unrepairable download has no general Give up action while known completed/ingesting. Options: repair-only behavior, or exact acknowledged discard retaining charge/completion/history and removing only publication/blocking authority. Recommend the latter as a separately designed command and UI contract. Never relabel completed work unknown to reuse release.
4. **Plausible vendor-liveness gap:** a retained vendor task may report accepted/running forever. Current release intentionally rejects non-unknown phases. Options: keep polling, or allow acknowledged detachment while preserving liability/reconciliation and late historical results. Recommend the latter; do not infer failure/refund from elapsed time.
5. **Confirmed existing DSL local-work limitation:** dispatched local timeline/render with no receipt terminates `LOCAL_EXECUTION_INTERRUPTED`, and identical-content admission cannot rerender. Recommend a fresh human retry identity with a final receipt check, retaining old dispatch evidence. Current storyboard export already permits a fresh-key new job; no automatic DSL retry was added.
6. **Plausible settings/legacy-plan availability limitations:** raw model-settings preview composition can likely refuse a mixed historical picture graph after unrelated accepted prompt edits. Optional legacy narration timeline rebase can similarly require plan review; canonical narration still commits and current storyboard export remains available. Options: keep safe refusal/notice, or reuse exact historical proof with explicit selected recipe/model effects. Recommend the latter only after deciding whether selected pending work should preserve the saved recipe or adopt current writing. Neither scenario was newly reproduced; no grants or execution arise from the refusal.
7. **Historical data limits:** revision-only note bases cannot retroactively detect narration/membership changes absent an exact original baseline; preserve their prior fence, and use digests for new/reopened bases. Existing immutable `tool_reconciliation:needs_followup` rows are not rewritten by the history fix. Stores already over Viggle's 4096 observation bound need a separate reviewed repair/retention design; X1 does not delete immutable evidence. The supplied snapshot has only three Viggle observations and is below that bound. Distinct genuinely changing vendor observations still require a long-term retention policy.
8. **Verification boundary:** no browser or live provider/Codex run was made. Rendered sample-film parity belongs to the lead under RULES. Fixed startup paths prove safe propagation with fake RPC/OS fixtures, not live-provider success or the root cause of the snapshot's historical eight failures.
9. **Process decisions:** ordinary human scene/audio completion still releases its own completed request holds. Only reused original Director conversation publication retains its explicitly unselected remainder. Historical tool matching is receipt identity recognition only; it does not authorize a runtime, tool invocation or new paid operation. No question or approval was needed.

## What the sample film must mirror

- A whole-film active Director request makes keyframes for one scene or shot while other scene/same-scene-sibling holds and already granted candidates stay blocked. Sequential scene decisions release only each selected part. Replay changes no grants or holds.
- Attached-recording transcription within that same request preserves unfinished picture-edit holds; ordinary standalone human audio completion still closes its own hold. Show the exact original transcription effect/attempt identity and V1 failure/release states.
- Accepted picture edits followed by standalone transcription preserve the old unselected graph, nodes, gates, candidates and media. Show current transcription authority/quote, and refuse missing/forged history. Do not rewrite old pictures to make the fixture pass.
- New blocking notes become stale on narration-only edits or cross-scene moves despite unchanged picture revisions; sibling notes stay current. Proposal accept→Undo reopens a fresh anchored note. Do not fabricate digests for historical revision-only bases.
- Repeated unknowns, local repair waits, normalization contention and unchanged pending/unknown video polls do not grow identical history across reopen. New trace IDs alone do not imply new task state. Changed status/errors/retry timing remain observable.
- Native config/auth/spawn refusal before dispatch shows the existing safe category and uncharged non-dispatch metadata, with no automatic retry/API fallback. Primary auth/protocol reason survives cleanup failure; successful checks plus failed cleanup stay blocked. Genuine post-marker unknown retains Give up and liability.
- Lost synchronous replies are failed/charged/no-auto-retry; a fresh human press uses a new identity. Definite no-start is uncharged; known late results after release remain historical. No fixture should discover a vendor task by application attempt ID.
- Historical committed tool receipts can settle a lost reply under their exact shipped predecessor identity without rerunning the tool. Tampered/unknown locks remain unresolved. Keep all broader Open issues visible rather than simulating backend capabilities not implemented here.
