# Sources

Researched October 2026. Most web material on video formats comes from vendors and agencies; treat their numbers as conventions, not research. Platform figures change often; verify current specs before relying on them. Structures such as three-act, AIDA, PAS and the trailer three-act are standard production practice and are drawn from general craft knowledge where not cited.
