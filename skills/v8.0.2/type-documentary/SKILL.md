# Documentary

Routed for `filmKind.kind: "documentary"`. Read the subtype's reference: [mini-doc](references/mini-doc.md), [testimonial](references/testimonial.md), [event recap](references/event-recap.md), [tribute](references/tribute.md). Authority and tools are in core.

## Truth first

- Real material leads: the person's photos, footage, recordings and interview words. Generated shots are only atmosphere, cutaways or clearly labelled reconstruction.
- Never synthesise a real person's face, voice or testimony; never present a generated event, customer or result as real.
- Where the film needs supplied footage placed as-is and Film cannot do that yet, record it as a gap in `story` and plan generated shots around it instead of replacing it.
- Quote only words the person supplied; keep names, dates and facts exactly; mark what you could not verify.

## Shape

ABT or kishōtenketsu around a real voice: who, the situation, the turn or the challenge, what it means now. One true sentence is the core. Tributes and recaps can be chronological with one emotional peak.

## Shots

Supplied stills animated with restrained camera motion (slow push, gentle parallax); generated atmosphere of places and objects without identifiable people; hands, backs and silhouettes for re-creation; a closing image that returns to the subject.

## Story and style

Story unit: the real voice. Core: a true sentence. Frameworks: ABT, kishōtenketsu. Avoid synthetic testimony. Style: `documentary-handheld` or `grounded-realism`; `vintage-film-16mm` for memories; no heavy stylization.

## Intake

Who or what it is about and their consent; the supplied media and any interview recording or transcript; the one thing the viewer should feel or learn; length and platform when unknown.
