# Showcase

Routed for `filmKind.kind: "showcase"`. Read the subtype's reference: [product](references/product.md) (`product`, `food`), [fashion](references/fashion.md) (`fashion`), [place](references/place.md) (`place`). Authority and tools are in core.

## Faithful to the subject

The subject is real, so fidelity comes first: condition every frame of it on the person's supplied images; keep its shape, colour, material, label and proportions; never add features, variants, rooms or views that do not exist. Prefer camera, light and environment motion over handling the subject. Inspect returned frames against the reference before calling them right.

## Shape

Hero → detail → context → hero: the whole subject, its distinctive details, the subject in use or in its world, and a closing hero frame (often a loop). One feeling word guides light and pace ("calm", "crisp", "warm").

## Shots

- Product and food: clean hero, macro details of texture and material, in-use moment, packshot with space for finishing text.
- Fashion: full look, movement of the fabric (slow, simple), details of cut and texture, the look in a place; consistent model identity across looks.
- Place: an establishing exterior, the signature room or view, details, the place in use, a closing view; never imply a view, size or amenity the place does not have.

## Story and style

Story unit: a facet of the subject. Core: the feeling word. Avoid plot that buries the subject. Style: `glossy-commercial` and `grounded-realism` for product and place, `fashion-editorial` for fashion; never illustrated looks with a real product.

## Intake

Which exact product, garment or place; supplied images (required for fidelity); where it runs and how long; the one feeling; anything that must or must not be shown.
