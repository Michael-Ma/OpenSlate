# Trailer

Routed for `filmKind.kind: "trailer"` (`trailer`, `teaser`). Read [the guide](references/guide.md) before planning. Authority and tools are in core.

## Shape

- Hook → setup of the world → escalation in three rising steps → the question → title and date.
- Hint, never reveal: no ending, no twist, no solution. The strongest image goes just before the title.
- `teaser`: shorter and stranger; one image or idea, a single question, the title.
- Cutting accelerates toward the climax; a beat of silence or a held image before the title.

## Shots

World-establishing images, the protagonist's want in one frame, escalating stakes, a mysterious or iconic image, the title frame with clean space. The title, date and any copy are finishing text, not generated pixels; plan the title frame as a clean image.

## Sound

Music builds in steps with a drop or a hit before the title; set it with `set_soundtrack` when the person supplies a track. Narration is sparse: three to five short lines rising in stakes (`craft-narration`).

## Fidelity

When promoting a real work, use the person's supplied stills and art as references; never invent characters, scenes or claims the work does not contain, and never imitate a real studio's branding.

## Story and style

Story unit: the escalating hint. Core: the question. Framework: hook → escalation → title. Avoid spoiling the ending. Style: follow the promoted work's look; otherwise `vintage-film-35mm`, `noir-bw` or `glossy-commercial`.

## Intake

What is promoted and when it comes out; supplied stills, art or footage; the tone; length and platform when unknown.
