# Vertical platforms

All of these are 9:16 at 1080×1920 on the viewer's phone; OpenSlate exports 720×1280 (clips are generated at 768p, so a larger export adds no detail).

| Platform | Typical organic length | UI over the frame | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 15–30 s performs best; longer allowed | Caption and audio line at the bottom, action rail on the right, header at the top | Ranks on watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach; label realistic AI video |
| Instagram Stories | Up to 15 s per card | Reply bar at the bottom, progress bar and avatar at the top | Default length band up to 15 s |
| TikTok | 15–30 s for most posts | Caption and sound at the bottom, action rail on the right | State the proposition in the first 3 s; label AI-generated content |
| YouTube Shorts | Up to 3 min; 15–40 s typical | Title and channel at the bottom, actions on the right | Loops replay automatically |
| Douyin, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels | 15–60 s | Similar bottom and right overlays | Write prompts and any voice line in Chinese when the film's language is Chinese |

## Safe zones (approximate)

- Top 14 %: platform header and status bar.
- Bottom 35 %: caption, audio, comment bar.
- Right 21 % below mid-height: like, comment, share, profile.
- Keep faces, product, the punch and any planned finishing text inside the remaining centre. The centre 3:4 crop (1080×1440) is what grids and previews show; the frame-1 subject must sit there.

## Composition patterns that work in portrait

- Single subject, chest-up close-up, eyes in the upper third of the safe zone.
- Subject and cause stacked: the man in the lower middle, the goose on the shelf above his shoulder.
- Low or high angles that use the height (a dog in a doorway from below, a desk from above).
- Reveals by vertical camera moves: tilt up to the cause, pull back to show the room.

## Things that fail in portrait

- Two people side by side at full width (one gets cut by the action rail).
- Landscapes and wide establishing shots with a small subject.
- Text near the bottom (hidden under the caption).
- Small props that must read at phone size.

## Mistakes

- A repurposed horizontal composition with a tiny subject.
- A slow logo or title intro.
- A long setup before the payoff.
- Several ideas in one post.
- A punch that needs a caption.
