# App / software promo

## Purpose

Show a digital product's value: the problem it removes, the moment it helps, and how simple it is. Success: the viewer understands what the app does and what it feels like to use, and nothing shown misrepresents the real interface.

## Format

- App store preview: 15–30 s. Launch/landing video: 30–90 s. Social ad: 15–30 s.
- App store previews use device aspect (portrait phone or landscape); landing pages 16:9; social 9:16.

## Structure

- **Problem moment → the app in hand → 2–3 key flows (each: action → result) → outcome in the user's life → CTA.**
- Lead with the user's outcome, not the login screen.
- Each feature is one flow in one scene.

## Scenes and shots

- 30 s: 3–4 scenes, 7–10 shots. 60 s: 5–6 scenes, 12–18 shots.
- Per flow: context shot (person, place, moment) + screen shot (real UI) + result/reaction shot.

## Pacing

- Brisk; each flow under 10 s. Hold result screens long enough to read the key element (2–3 s).

## Must-have shots

- Relatable problem moment in the real world.
- Device in hand or on desk in a believable context.
- Real UI for each key flow, from supplied screenshots or recordings.
- Result in the world (arrived on time, organised, paid).
- Logo/app icon end frame with store badges added in finishing.

## What matters most

- UI truthfulness and legibility.
- Clear human benefit, not a tour of menus.
- Brand colour continuity between UI and world.

## Narration, text and music

- Voiceover or captions naming each benefit in a short phrase.
- Feature labels, store badges and CTA as finishing text.
- Upbeat, clean music.

## Common mistakes

- A screen-by-screen tour of every feature.
- Tiny UI that cannot be read on a phone.
- Fictional features or screens.
- No human context.

## Generation caveats

- **Do not generate app screens.** Generated UI shows garbled text, wrong layouts and invented features. Use supplied screenshots or screen recordings for every screen; generated imagery covers only real-world context.
- Device screens in generated shots: keep them dark, angled away, or reflected, and composite the real UI in finishing; note the composite as a gap if the pipeline cannot do it.
- Typing and swiping hands are fragile; show the result instead of the input, or one simple tap.
- Store badges and logos must use official files in finishing.
- Platform rules for app store previews (often requiring real captured app footage) may forbid generated content; check before planning a store preview.

## Intake questions

1. What does the app do, and which 2–3 flows matter most?
2. Please attach screenshots or screen recordings of those flows, plus app icon and logo.
3. Where will it run (app store preview, website, social ad), at what length? (Default: 30 s 16:9 plus 9:16 cut.)
4. Who is the target user, and in what moment of their day do they use it?
