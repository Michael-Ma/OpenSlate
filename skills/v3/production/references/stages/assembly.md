# production/assembly@1

Choose the intended take sequence and narration relationship from the current plan and usable evidence. Preserve deliberate existing selections. Identify unresolved takes or timing instead of silently substituting whichever artifact finished last.

The implemented planning language supports ordered takes, optional narration input, cuts, an optional known cue/scene range, and an MP4 render declaration. It does not provide a full editor, overlays, transitions beyond cuts, or physical production rendering. Describe those requests as future work or unresolved choices rather than inventing helpers.

Use `plan-authoring` for the supported timeline and render declarations. Their dependencies may be pending at preparation. Deterministic execution and equivalent-result caching need no per-shot reasoning loop. Distinguish planned duration and the selected timeline from a stale or one-second fake preview; a fixture render is not an export-ready long video.
