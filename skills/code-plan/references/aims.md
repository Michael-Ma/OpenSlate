# Aims

What the viewer comes for: the response the last picture should leave. Choose one main aim, which owns the last picture, and at most one second aim, which carries the build: a gag that turns tender is Laugh, then Moved. A sad build under a warm ending blends well; a happy build under fear or disgust does not.

**Reading the aim from a brief:**

- funny, a gag, 搞笑, 沙雕: Laugh;
- scary, creepy, 恐怖, 惊悚, 紧张: Suspense;
- a twist, "turns out", 反转, 神转折: Twist;
- heartwarming, kind, cute, 暖心, 治愈, 萌: Moved;
- goodbye, memory, childhood, 怀念, 遗憾, 泪目: Bittersweet;
- proud, never give up, 燃, 励志, 致敬: Inspire;
- revenge, comeback, 逆袭, 打脸, 爽: Payback;
- epic, magical, surreal, 震撼, 奇幻: Wonder;
- quiet, relaxing, rain, 安静, 氛围: Calm;
- delicious, premium, a dream trip, 馋, 高级感, 种草: Crave;
- satisfying, a process, before and after, 解压, 过程: Satisfying;
- fast, action, sport, dance, 动作, 卡点: Thrill;
- how, why, 科普, 原理, 干货: Understand;
- a warning, a cause, 公益, 警示: Concern.

With no cue: a product or food is Crave; a person or an animal is Moved; a place without people is Calm, or Wonder when the brief sounds large.

**Neighbours, told apart by the ending.** Crave: the viewer wants to have it; Satisfying: they relish that it is finished. Wonder: the eyes widen; Calm: the breath slows. Moved: closeness restored; Bittersweet: an absence. Moved: they want to hold someone; Inspire: they want to do something. Inspire ends on the one who rose; Payback on the face of the one who scorned. Suspense: they feared the outcome all along; Twist: they read the film again after the last picture. A reveal that is harmless and absurd is a Laugh.

Each shape is a starting point. Use one, bend one, cross two, or leave them all when the brief gives you something better, and say which in the take's `basis` ("template: <name>", "bent: <name>" or "own"). At least one of your three takes owes nothing to these files. Used straight, every shape is somebody's cliché; to bend one, change who does it, where the turn falls or what the last picture is. Each line lists its blocks in order and **turn** marks the fulcrum. The timeline only cuts: no dissolves, wipes, split screens or reversed clips.

## Moves for any aim

- A thing used or treated as another thing.
- A framing that lets the viewer assume one thing until a wider or later picture shows another.
- The last character you would expect to do this; power given to the one who should not have it.
- Another kind of film played completely straight: a trailer, a nature documentary or a heist, for something small.
- A phrase or idiom happening for real.
- A shift of scale: the tiny treated as vast, the vast as tiny.
- The same frame at another time, or one thing multiplied.
- One object that carries the film and comes back changed.
- An image that seems unrelated until the ending links it.
- The viewer knowing what the character does not.
- The last picture cutting back to the first, which now means something else.

## Laugh

The viewer laughs or smirks. Gag comedy runs on one mechanism and lives at the cut; character comedy runs on someone who wants something and keeps their dignity, and lives in reactions. Good, not generic: everyone in the film takes it seriously; exaggerate, since "normal speed" erases a joke; one mechanism only; harmless, more humiliated than hurt; the result registers on a held face or picture. The turn falls late, with a short button after it.

- **Switch image.** A tight framing reads as grief, danger or romance; hold until it is believed; **turn:** a wider picture shows the innocent truth. Stale: "it was a dream". Bend: reframe the reframe.
- **Wrong solution, in earnest.** A small problem; the character fixes it the hard, absurd way with full concentration; **turn:** the result, and a face that finds it reasonable.
- **Three, then the break.** A pattern in one framing, three times, each a little bigger (reuse the clip, punch in); **turn:** the fourth breaks it. Bend: the fourth is smaller, or nothing happens.
- **Dignity against the world.** A composed character attempts one small proud task; the world defeats it on a cut or by a sound; **turn:** the face that does not move; they carry on. Bend: they win by accident and accept the credit gravely.
- **Animal with a plan.** The animal wants one thing; a polite attempt; a bolder one; **turn:** its ruthless solution, told by the result and the human's reaction. The animal keeps the dignity.
- **Grand build, small payoff.** Epic light, scale and music promise an event; it builds; **turn:** the thing is tiny or ordinary, and the music stops.
- **Exit, changed return.** The character leaves a locked frame with purpose; the empty frame holds over a sound; **turn:** they come back soaked, chased or carrying something. Bend: three returns, each calmer.
- **Look, realise, drop.** Confident action; a freeze as the character notices; a held look; **turn:** a cut to the small, distant result.

## Suspense

The viewer fears what is about to happen, because they know more than the character does. Good, not generic: one firm rule for the threat; empty space and silence in place of a sting; stop early. It rises throughout and releases, or strikes, in the last seconds.

- **Bomb under the table.** Show the danger; the character does something ordinary, slowly; cut back to the danger, closer each time; **turn:** the character almost notices. Bend: the threat is harmless and still escalates.
- **Rule of the threat.** Show when it appears (only in the dark, only in reflections); repeat until the viewer has learned it; **turn:** the condition returns, nearer; the rule breaks. One place in two states of light.
- **Threat by sound.** Quiet; one sound; a listening face, then the empty doorway; **turn:** the sound again, nearer; cut out. Bend: the sound is a pleasant one.
- **Stop before the answer.** Something inexplicable; a slow approach; **turn:** cut a beat before it is seen, or on a clue that changes the question. The question must be specific.

## Twist

The viewer is jolted into reading the film again. Withhold one fact, plant fair clues, and let the last picture recast what came before; a twist without a plant is a cheat. The turn is the last fifth.

- **Ordinary turned wrong.** A warm routine; small details gather; **turn:** one last readable picture recasts them. Stale: "dead all along". Bend: a figure of speech made literal.
- **Thing in the background.** A calm wide picture; a cutaway; **turn:** the same picture with one thing added or missing; hold while the viewer searches.
- **Second look.** A picture, read one way; the story; **turn:** the same picture again, where a detail now means the opposite.
- **Replay with the signs.** A sweet story in the foreground; **turn:** it ends badly; the same clips again, punched in on what we missed.
- **Who it was for.** We follow one person's effort; **turn:** the last picture shows whose story it was.

## Moved

The viewer feels a warm lump in the throat when a gap between people, or between a person and an animal, suddenly closes. Good, not generic: feeling the film has earned through one specific act or object; cut away before the tears; leave one thing open. Stale everywhere: an underdog, slow motion and a neat reward. The turn falls at about two thirds, then a last picture that lingers.

- **Ritual with a change.** A small ritual in one framing; again, later (the light, the season); **turn:** again, with the roles swapped: the child now does it for the parent.
- **The misread wish.** A character waits, impatient, and we read it as selfish; it grows; **turn:** they give the thing away.
- **Kindness nobody sees.** A small unrewarded act; repeated; **turn:** what grew from it, shown without the character. End on one result, not three.
- **Reaching the lonely one.** Distance between two; attempts fail; **turn:** the gesture arrives, seen on the receiver's face. Bend: the giver is lonely too.
- **The small one tries.** Something small attempts what is too big for it; it fails, sweetly; **turn:** the one it was copying helps without a word; they rest together.

## Bittersweet

The viewer aches at loss, time or longing. Establish something precious, show its absence or its passing, and resolve nothing; end on an empty frame or a held object. Nostalgia is the kind where what is gone is the viewer's own past. The turn is late and quiet, never a shock.

- **Object for the person.** An ordinary object, seen with care; someone handles it; **turn:** the object where it tells us its owner is gone. Bend: it is still in use, as when two cups are set out from habit.
- **Ritual, one missing.** A small ritual for two, in one framing; again, later; **turn:** again, alone.
- **A life in traces.** Things left behind tell it (tickets, a ring box, a crib); they gather; **turn:** one last trace jumps years.
- **Then and now.** A place or a pose, then; the same framing, now; **turn:** what is no longer in it.
- **Trace of someone.** The camera stays outside the event; a thing left behind (a wet umbrella, a warm cup); the place without them; **turn:** the first picture again, now meaning more.

## Inspire

The viewer feels pride or resolve and wants to act. Admiration, for excellence through effort, makes them want to improve; elevation, at moral beauty, makes them want to help. Earned, not empty: show the cost (time, ridicule, loneliness) before the payoff, and make sure the claim fits whoever is making it. The turn falls at about two thirds and builds to a raised, forward-facing picture.

- **Manifesto roll-call.** A creed in voice-over over people who look odd; each one changed something; **turn:** the line turns to "you". Bend: ordinary anonymous people, and the last place empty.
- **Dark before light.** Lonely, repeated work in the dark (reuse one clip); it goes on; **turn:** a hard cut to light and sound. Bend: the payoff is tiny, one lit pool at dawn.
- **The unseen supporter.** Someone trains; someone else does the small invisible work; **turn:** the win, and who it was for. Bend: the supporter stays off screen, only their traces.
- **The flipped insult.** The mocking version of an act; the young do it with full force; **turn:** a question redefines the phrase.
- **Two portraits.** How they describe themselves; how a stranger does; **turn:** the two, side by side by a hard cut. Bend: a place or a craft, not a face.
- **Many as one.** People apart; pairs whose movements match across cuts; **turn:** everyone together.
- **Dare ladder.** A small brave act; a bigger one; **turn:** what it cost the one who is speaking.

## Payback

爽: the viewer relishes seeing someone wronged proved right, and the scorner fall. Wrong the hero clearly, let contempt mount, then let one visible proof turn the room, and end on the scorner's face. Its fullest shapes are the Micro drama structures in [formats](formats.md): humiliation and slap-back, hidden identity, second life, proof object. Stale: the black card, the kneeling crowd. Bend: the hero reveals nothing and the scorner's own object betrays them.

- **The last laugh.** A habit or a skill everyone mocks; a crisis; **turn:** it is the only thing that works; the mockers' faces.
- **Quiet exit.** They are dismissed; they leave without a word; **turn:** what they leave behind shows what has been lost.

## Wonder

The viewer is struck by something vast or impossible, and the mind has to stretch to take it in. Spectacle builds by accumulation and needs no twist; the surreal breaks one rule of the world and needs a turn. Good, not generic: an idea sayable in two lines; exactly one broken rule, played straight; it looks as if it happened in front of a camera.

- **One impossible rule.** Everyday life, filmed plainly; **turn:** the one wrong thing; the world treats it as normal; a small consequence of the rule. Stale: a surreal pile-up.
- **Scale ladder.** A small human scene; each cut is much wider, or much closer, in a rhythm the viewer learns; **turn:** the scale passes the human; a snap back to the start.
- **Flood of simple things.** A quiet real place; the first few arrive; **turn:** the flood, slow; one small detail, then stillness. Avoid counts and collisions. Bend: an unexpected material, or a sad place.
- **Change in place.** One framing and one composition; after each cut the subject is another thing; the chain quickens; **turn:** it lands on the one that means something.
- **Journey backwards.** A familiar present; the surroundings regress; the travellers change too; **turn:** the oddest end state, then a button. Bend: forwards, into an absurd future.

## Calm

The viewer settles and breathes more slowly. Nothing has to happen, but something has to change. Good, not merely pretty: two images that answer each other, one small change, and a pause placed on purpose. A list of nice shots is not a film.

- **Two images and a cut.** A broad image fixes season and place; **turn:** one small close image that the first recolours; hold. Bend: a contrast of scale or temperature.
- **Four parts, one stranger** (起承转合). A place; it develops; **turn:** an image that seems unrelated; a last picture links it by shape or colour, never by explanation.
- **Pillow sequence.** The outside of a place; closer, empty rooms and things; **turn:** one held object that has become charged. Bend: one sign of life enters.
- **A day in one place.** One locked frame: dawn, empty; midday, life at a distance; **turn:** the lamps come on; night, empty again. Bend: a single room, or the day in reverse order.
- **Weather arrives.** Stillness; signs (wind in the grass, the light dims); **turn:** the first drops; full weather, then a pause. Bend: what the weather leaves behind.

## Crave

The viewer wants it: to taste it, own it or be there. Appetite sells texture and sensation; aspiration sells a mood with the object held back. Good, not generic: physical imperfection (condensation, a crack, an unruly splash); a cue of the object, its colour or one part, in the first two seconds; no invented claims. The hero picture comes in the last quarter: the object alone in its light, held two to three seconds.

- **Hero in light.** Edge glints in darkness; light and a slow move reveal form and material; **turn:** the whole object reads; a detail, then the object held. Bend: it is lit by something from its own world.
- **Crave sequence.** Macro texture; one action (a pour, a break, steam); **turn:** the inside revealed; the hero plate, still. Cut from before the contact to the opened result, with a sound. Stale: bite and smile. Bend: no person, or only hands.
- **Mood first, object last.** A world or a feeling; it deepens; **turn:** the object appears as its source; the object inside the mood. Seed its colour or shape in the first cut.
- **Benefit as spectacle.** An ordinary place; a phenomenon begins; **turn:** its full scale; a quiet detail. The phenomenon is the benefit, literally.

## Satisfying

The viewer feels the click of completion. Completion has hands in it; time has none; the destruction of order (a clean cut through, a pour over) satisfies too. Good, not generic: a clear "just right" endpoint, which is the last frame; the before and the after in the same framing; a sound on every contact.

- **Raw to finished.** Raw material; three to five steps, each one simple action; **turn:** the step where it first looks like the thing; the finished object in use. Show the state before and after each contact and cut across it. Bend: follow one object's journey.
- **Overhead assembly.** One locked top-down frame; layers appear as stills; **turn:** the money shot (a cut, a pour); the finished thing, held. Bend: open on the finished picture.
- **Ruin to restored.** A wreck, framed tight; fragments of work (sparks, sanding, polish); **turn:** a match cut to the restored thing in the identical framing; it works. Bend: restore a place.
- **Time in one frame.** A locked frame; seasons or growth pass as stills from one composition; **turn:** the peak; return or rest. Bend: one human-scale thing that changes against them.
- **Chain reaction.** A first nudge; links, one clip each, cut on the impact with a sound; **turn:** the improbable link; the last trigger, then silence. Bend: natural links such as wind, water, light and animals.
- **Built before your eyes.** Curious parts; assembly quickens, one action a cut; **turn:** the last piece, and the whole is recognised. Bend: it is made from what it promises.

## Thrill

The viewer's pulse rises with speed and rhythm. Pursuit builds by complications; a montage builds in waves. Good, not generic: the subject in the centre of every frame, so the eye never hunts; waves with a reset before the biggest, not one ramp; a sound on the cuts that matter. It ends on a peak.

- **Centre-framed pursuit.** The goal and the threat; the pursuer closes; **turn:** a complication flips the advantage; escape or catch. Motion leads into each cut. Bend: a slow pursuer, such as a tide.
- **Disadvantage and hit.** The hero is weaker; they notice a thing in the place; **turn:** the hit, cut before contact and shown again punched in; the reaction earns the finish.
- **Waves.** A rise; a reset; a bigger rise; **turn:** a hard stop of silence; the biggest wave; a button.
- **Sample montage.** A process as quick simple actions, each with a sound; the repeats build (reuse clips, speed them up); **turn:** the loop breaks or mutates.
- **Rhyme cuts.** Pairs from different worlds whose motions match across the cut; the pairs quicken; **turn:** a pair that reads as one continuous movement. Bend: species, or eras.

## Understand

The viewer gets it: "now I see". Proof shows it (a demonstration, a comparison); insight reframes it (an analogy, a scale, a belief overturned). Good, not generic: the viewer predicts before the answer comes; one idea a film; the proof is seen, not told. No numbers or labels inside pictures. The turn is the demonstration, at about two thirds.

- **Vivid demonstration.** A claim we doubt; the test; **turn:** the result, held. Test a change of material, not a count. Bend: the test happens somewhere absurd.
- **Torture test.** The stakes made visible; the test, in one slow wide shot; **turn:** the instant it should fail and does not, on a cutaway; a calm aftermath. Bend: nature is the torturer.
- **Misconception overturned.** "Obviously…", said for the viewer; they commit; **turn:** reality differs. The film must voice the wrong idea itself.
- **Scale made visible.** A human-sized frame; step out, and out again; **turn:** the vastness lands. Bend: go inwards.
- **Analogy that clicks.** A familiar object; "this is your…", said once; **turn:** watch what happens to it. One mapping only.
- **Hidden mechanism.** A blur we cannot read; **turn:** slowed, frozen or cut away; back to speed, now legible.
- **Side by side.** Two identical starts; one thing differs; **turn:** they diverge. Light or colour marks the difference, never a label.

## Concern

The viewer is unsettled or angered into caring. A film about personal risk asks them to change what they do; a film about a cause asks for their support. A threat works only with one thing the viewer can do about it; without that they look away. Preachy is a lecture, an accusation and no action: lead with a story, delay the cause, and end on one small specific act. Restraint beats shock: what the viewer imagines is worse.

- **Replay with the signs.** A sweet story; **turn:** violence; the same clips again, with each sign we missed; learn the signs.
- **Genre hijack.** A cheerful, familiar kind of advert; item by item it becomes the issue; **turn:** the music cuts; one quiet picture.
- **It could be here.** An ordinary life in one recurring framing; the background darkens; **turn:** the far-off crisis is this life. Change the light, the weather and the set, never the framing.
- **Embodied metaphor.** A gentle scene; the danger, slowed; **turn:** the people who love them become the protection. No words; one caption at the end.
- **Cute catalogue.** A charming list of silly harms; **turn:** the real one, named last and kept off screen; a pledge.
- **Impossible body.** A strange figure, calmly shown; why it looks like this; **turn:** we are not built for it.
