# production/story@1

Develop a clear progression from the settled brief or narration: what the viewer understands first, what develops, and what the ending resolves or asks them to do. Give each beat a visible purpose. Keep claims consistent with supplied facts; do not invent product promises for dramatic effect.

Use the supported `story` text to state the beats, emotional direction, and recurring visual idea. Match ambition to the duration and available assets. If a final narration already establishes the story, adapt its visual treatment rather than replacing its wording.

Draft through nonessential uncertainty with stated assumptions. Ask about a material conflict between the user's goals. A structured story draft is useful evidence, not automatically an accepted screenplay or a requirement to run every earlier stage again.
