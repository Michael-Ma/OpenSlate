# production/storyboard@1

Prepare one conditioning image per shot requiring video, using the current shot intent and registered references. A usable imported frame may be reused after the application's normalization and review requirements; an existing frame's mere presence does not imply approval.

Choose composition, subject visibility, lighting, and framing that support the planned movement. Keep references and prompts consistent with adjacent shots. Use the plan-authoring grammar to declare image work and exact review membership. A generated frame may be a future symbolic input while the plan is prepared.

Present the actual resulting frame and intended motion/duration for human review. Do not perform hidden image changes after approval or create variants merely because you dislike the result. Pending narration timing may allow image preparation; it does not waive later video readiness checks.
