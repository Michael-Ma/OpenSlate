# production/assembly@1

Use Film to inspect the current storyboard-order clips, narration, soundtrack and export readiness. Use Assets for saved media. Preserve deliberate take selections and exact audio/placement lineage. Identify unresolved or outdated media instead of choosing whichever artifact finished last.

Film exports are assembled by the application; ordinary export needs no Director-authored plan. If separate timeline/render source is actually needed, use `plan-authoring` for only those operations over verified owned media. Never author image, review or video declarations or `requestNewTakes`, and never combine creative edits with source in one preparation. Keep unsupported overlays/transitions as future work rather than inventing helpers.

The executor handles scheduling, caching and recovery without per-shot reasoning loops. A successful fake preview does not establish a real export's duration, readiness or quality. Report actual artifact evidence.
