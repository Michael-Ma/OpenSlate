# Story frameworks

Pick one framework per film and name it in `story`. Each beat gets one job; each job becomes a scene or shot `purpose`.

| Framework | Use for | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Story Spine | narrative, kids, tribute | Once upon a time / Every day / Until one day / Because of that (×n) / Until finally / Ever since then |
| ABT (And, But, Therefore) | explainer, brand film, mini-doc, narrated story | And: the situation. But: the problem. Therefore: the resolution |
| Single-minded proposition + proof | commercial, showcase | One claim, two or three proof images, one closing action |
| Hook → promise → body → payoff → loop | social, trailer | 0–2 s hook; the promise by 3 s; payoff on a cut; last frame returns to the first |
| Rule + variation + escalation | music video concept mode, visual poem | One visual rule; each chorus changes one dimension |
| Kishōtenketsu (起承转合) | kids, brand, tribute; stories without conflict | Introduce / develop / twist (an unexpected juxtaposition) / reconcile |
| Logline + treatment | music video, trailer | Who / what / where in one sentence, then a 150–300-word treatment |
| List or countdown | narrated story (top-N) | Weakest to strongest; the last item calls back to the first |
| Setup → build → beat before → punch → button | comedy in any type | See `craft-comedy` |

## Worked shapes

**Story Spine, 45 s, five scenes.** Every day the lighthouse keeper polishes the lamp (routine). Until one day the lamp will not light (break). Because of that she climbs out with a lantern (choice). Until finally a ship turns in safely (climax). Ever since then she keeps a lantern by the door (new routine, mirrored image).

**ABT, 30 s explainer.** And: your files live on three laptops. But: nobody knows which copy is current. Therefore: one shared folder that always shows the latest version. One shot per clause, plus a hero shot of the result.

**Proposition + proof, 15 s ad.** Proposition: "Coffee that stays hot through your commute." Proof 1: steam still rising at the bus stop. Proof 2: the last sip at the office desk, still steaming. Close: the cup on the desk with space for the end card.

**Hook → loop, 20 s social.** Hook: the odd detail in frame 1 (a goose by the self-checkout, unexplained). Build: the situation escalates. Payoff: the reveal on a cut. Loop: the last frame cuts back to frame 1.

**Rule + escalation, music video.** Rule: the singer stands still while the room around her changes per section. Verse: the room empties. Chorus 1: it floods with light. Chorus 2: with people. Final chorus: all three at once.

**Kishōtenketsu, 40 s tribute.** Introduce: grandfather's hands repairing a radio. Develop: the workshop through the years. Twist: the granddaughter's hands, same bench, a phone. Reconcile: both sets of hands in one frame, the radio playing.

## Short-length limits

- Under 20 s: one premise, one turn at most; show a state contrast rather than an arc.
- 20–60 s: one character change at most; two or three beats of development.
- Over 60 s: a full spine is possible; keep one through-line and one motif.
