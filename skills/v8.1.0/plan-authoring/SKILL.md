# OpenSlate plan authoring

Ordinary creative planning saves writing, not executable generation source. Read [the current guidance and the creative-patch example](references/grammar.md) with `read_guidance` when you write a patch or source; it is one file. Follow the production package's Reply style and authority rules; they take precedence. Obtain current head/revision, saved IDs, media and settled choices from paged context; do not invent identities.

For clear typed proposal decisions in a fresh user editing turn, use `accept_proposal` / `reject_proposal` with `{proposalId}` as described in [the grammar](references/grammar.md). Identify the proposal by letter, subject or a clear reference to the only held proposal; if several are open and the message is ambiguous, ask which one. After success, report the decision and that accepted changes can be undone, then continue other requested work using the accepted prompts. Clips still to make require the clicked `confirm_video` card after frames are opened; a named saved-clip remake uses `remake_clip` and keeps its keyframe.

Never author image, review or video declarations (`p.image`, `p.humanReview`, `p.approvedImage`, `p.video`), and never use `requestNewTakes`. The application builds all keyframe and video work; `make_keyframes` requests keyframes and needed voice lines, while video requires human scene confirmation. Never combine creative edits with `source` in one `prepare_change`. Only separate timeline and render source remains available when actually needed; Film exports are assembled by the application without Director source, and `start_export` starts one.

For harmless writing, use `prepare_change` with `variant: "project"`, current `expectedHeadVersion` and `creative`, then apply its returned prepared ID. Create shots before reading their service-issued IDs. Use short scene `title` and descriptive `purpose`; put coverage, timing and narration range in `story`. Framing is a 1–3-word label of at most 40 characters (Close-up, Medium, Wide). `filmKind`, `visualStyle` and character writing follow the contract reference; character writing goes in its own call.

On shots with current or retained media, changes to purpose, action, framing, motion, prompts, duration or narration use `propose_change` and a note linking its proposalId. Retained voice or accepted cues also establish media impact. Pure text without media impact may apply directly with a receipt. Respect `PROPOSAL_REQUIRED` and human field conflicts; never split an edit to bypass them. The full 7.0.0 proposal fields are described in the grammar. Global canonical narration editing and invented acceptance/timing fields are unavailable.

When the person asks for keyframes in a fresh editing conversation, call `make_keyframes` within that scope; they may also press "Make keyframes" in Keyframes. `regenerate: true` requires explicit `shotIds` and a request for new takes. If the new-keyframe shots are unclear or you propose a new frame, offer a `regenerate_keyframe` note choice; existing takes stay in history. For clips still to make in ready scenes, record a scene-anchored `confirm_video` note choice for each, with `reply` when useful, and say: "Confirm in this chat or in Keyframes." They review exact frames, motion, narration and video recipe before the click. Report a start only after the application reports it. Reviews, review replies, automatic, discuss-only and read-only turns discuss the saved work and name the one step that needs the person; requested keyframes start from a fresh human editing request. An audio attachment sent with that request may be transcribed when its words are requested or needed; other audio tools save pending suggestions for a human press; never pass suggestions to `apply_change`. Configured keys need no approval or usage allowance.

Keep unchanged work and operation identities. After an uncertain response inspect receipts using the original identity and stop dependent changes if unresolved; never resubmit blindly. Refresh/reprepare actual conflicts, stop after revoked authority and preserve request-owned holds. The executor owns scheduling, technical retries and unknown submission reconciliation. Say what was made using application receipts; give creative observations only from inspected media.
