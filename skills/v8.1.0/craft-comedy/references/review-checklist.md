# Comedy review checklist

Run twice: on the written plan before keyframes, and on the returned keyframes and clips before saying the piece is ready. Answer each line yes or no. Any "no" in sections 1–3 means revise the plan, not the prompt wording. Report only the failures and the fix, in one or two lines each. Base clip judgements only on inspected media.

## 1. Is there a joke?

- [ ] I can say the premise in one sentence: target + violation.
- [ ] I can say why it is safe: nobody real is hurt.
- [ ] There is one premise, not two.
- [ ] The punch is a single image I can describe.
- [ ] The setup creates an expectation the punch breaks, and the punch makes sense in hindsight.
- [ ] Frame 1 does not reveal the punch.
- [ ] A friend told the premise in one line would smile. If it needs explaining, it is not ready.

## 2. Would someone laugh, and send it?

- [ ] Recognition: a real viewer would say "that is me / that is my dog / that is my mum".
- [ ] I can name who a viewer would DM it to.
- [ ] It escalates or repeats a pattern; the last beat is the biggest.
- [ ] The characters play it straight; nothing winks at the camera except a deliberate deadpan look.
- [ ] No line or shot explains the joke.

## 3. Is it kind?

- [ ] The butt of the joke is a situation, an object, a machine or the protagonist's own flaw.
- [ ] No group (race, ethnicity, religion, disability, body, gender, sexuality, age, class, accent) is the punchline; no stereotype carries the joke.
- [ ] No real person, celebrity or politician; no imitated real voice.
- [ ] No real brand, store, app, logo or product.
- [ ] No real injury, emergency, cruelty to animals, or copyable dangerous stunt; kids and animals are never in distress.
- [ ] Gestures, foods, dress and religious objects are checked for the audience's culture.

## 4. Does the timing work?

- [ ] Hook in the first second: subject, situation, odd detail, readable as a thumbnail.
- [ ] By 3 s the viewer knows the game.
- [ ] There is a beat before the punch (held look, stillness or music cut).
- [ ] The punch lands on a cut or a clear end state, not mid-drift.
- [ ] The reaction or result holds 1–2 s after the punch.
- [ ] Total 15–20 s; nothing can be cut without breaking the joke.
- [ ] The last frame cuts cleanly back to the first, or ends on a button.

## 5. Will it render?

- [ ] Every shot is one subject action and one camera move with a stated end state.
- [ ] The punch image is in a keyframe, not left to motion.
- [ ] Every key expression is in a keyframe and is one big, held expression.
- [ ] No readable text is needed: signs, screens, labels and captions are blank or absent.
- [ ] No lip-synced dialogue; any voice is off-face (VO, device, off-screen).
- [ ] No precise hand or contact gag (catching, pouring on target, typing).
- [ ] The recurring character is described with identical words in every prompt.
- [ ] Any sound effect that helps the punch is named in the motion prompt, and the joke still reads if the sound lands late. Clip sound is kept by default for social and comedy films.
- [ ] Faces and props sit in the safe middle band; top 15 % and bottom third are clear.

## 6. Sound

- [ ] Works muted.
- [ ] At most one VO line, 3–8 words, in the setup.
- [ ] Music has a planned change point (cut to silence or a tone switch) at the punch.
- [ ] No copyrighted or trending track is required.

## 7. After the clips return

- [ ] The expression in each clip still reads at phone size; it did not drift into a different emotion.
- [ ] The punch subject (goose, jungle, cat) is clearly visible and not deformed.
- [ ] Neighbouring shots match: same character, wardrobe and set.
- [ ] No artefact makes the viewer laugh at the AI instead of at the joke, unless that is the style.
- [ ] Watched once end to end with sound and once muted, I smiled at the punch. If not, name the weakest beat and offer a fix (re-take, reframe, or a sharper premise) as a `regenerate_keyframe` choice for a new frame, a `remake_clip` choice for named saved clips keeping their ready keyframes, or a proposal choice; never start a remake on your own.
- [ ] Realistic synthetic footage: remind the person once to post with the AI label.
