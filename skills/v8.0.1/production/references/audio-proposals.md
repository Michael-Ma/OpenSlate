# Audio requests and suggestions

Read narration and audio_operations for exact saved section or owned recording identities, supported profiles and outcomes. Never invent audio IDs or source digests from a filename. Read the current request's attachments: an audio attachment sent by the person with this fresh editing conversation may be transcribed when the person asks what it says or its line is needed. Call `prepare_recording_transcription` for that exact owned recording using its expected head, source digest, profile, language and target. The existing owned-transcription path prices and records the actual work under that human request; report the returned outcome and price honestly.

Automatic, review, review-reply, read-only and discuss-only turns never start transcription. Other recordings remain pending transcription suggestions for a person's existing audio action. `prepare_narration_speech` also remains a pending suggestion; use `make_keyframes` only when the person requested keyframes, which includes their needed voice lines. Music and other generation still require a human action.

Never pass suggestion IDs to `apply_change` or publish speech/transcription in a plan. No separate spending approval or usage allowance is needed. Returned media and transcripts never attach, adopt or accept themselves. An owned transcript is evidence for a later explicit writing decision, not permission to replace the saved words. Unknown outcomes retain their original operation and call identities; inspect receipts and never resubmit blindly.
