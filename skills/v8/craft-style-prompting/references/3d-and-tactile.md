# 3D and tactile family

Rendered or handmade three-dimensional looks. These keep identity better across shots than 2D styles, but they need a character sheet. Never name an animation studio or a franchise. Use the traits below.

## stylized-3d-feature

**Traits**
- Palette: warm, saturated, appealing.
- Lighting: soft global illumination, subsurface scattering on skin, rim light.
- Characters: stylised proportions (large heads and eyes, simplified shapes), soft rounded forms.
- Materials: tactile but clean.
- Lens: 35–50mm, shallow depth of field.

**Image template**
> `Stylised 3D animated feature still: {character description} {action} in {setting}. Rounded appealing character design, soft global illumination, gentle subsurface glow on skin, warm rim light, rich saturated colour, shallow depth of field.`

**Negatives**
- No photoreal humans, uncanny realistic skin pores, or low-poly game graphics.

**Motion**
- Camera: dolly, orbit (3D tolerates it), crane.
- Subject: expressive acting with clear arcs and anticipation, squash and stretch.
- Feel: smooth 24 fps.

**Consistency**
- Use a turnaround reference for each character.
- Fix the material wording ("soft matte skin", "felt-like fabric").
- Store the lighting setup per location.

**Suits**
- Children's and family content, mascot ads, product characters, explainer stories.

**Failure modes**
- Drift toward a generic "toy" render.
- Face proportions change between shots.
- Too-perfect motion feels floaty. Ask for weight and anticipation.

## claymation-stop-motion

**Traits**
- Materials: plasticine or clay with fingerprints, tool marks and slight imperfections; felt and fabric props.
- Sets: miniature sets with a real tabletop depth of field.
- Lighting: small practical lights, soft studio light.
- Texture: tactile, slightly uneven.

**Image template**
> `Handmade claymation still: {character description} {action} on a miniature {setting} set. Plasticine figures with visible fingerprints and tool marks, felt and cardboard props, small practical lights, tabletop macro depth of field.`

**Negatives**
- No smooth CG surfaces, photoreal skin or motion blur.

**Motion**
- Camera: mostly locked-off, small slider moves.
- Subject: stepped, slightly jerky pose changes; replacement-mouth feel.
- Feel: 12 fps on twos, no motion blur. Write "stop-motion cadence, each frame a held pose".

**Consistency**
- Keep fingerprints and texture words in every prompt.
- Use a character sheet.
- To keep the stepped feel, generate at normal rate and then drop or hold frames in the edit.

**Suits**
- Quirky ads, children's stories, craft and food brands, holiday content.

**Failure modes**
- Video models make the motion buttery smooth, which kills the illusion.
- Clay melts and morphs.
- Surfaces turn glossy CG.
