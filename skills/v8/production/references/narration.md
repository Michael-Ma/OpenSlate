# Narration decisions

Determine what the person supplied: final recording, partial recording, approved script, rough text or an idea. Inspect registered evidence. A filename, attachment mention, desired duration or reading estimate is not measured timing and does not prove that you heard a recording.

Preserve a final recording's words/performance and settled script, voice, language and pronunciation choices unless asked to change them. For partial material, identify missing passages without replacing settled passages. Ask only for a decision that affects the next useful step. Draft independent story work while a genuine audio gap remains.

Read `narration` to distinguish writing, bound recordings and measured cues, including unbound recordings. If recognition of an owned recording is explicitly requested, read `audio_operations` and prepare that exact transcription; only an eligible audio attachment sent with this fresh human editing request may start it immediately, otherwise it remains a suggestion for a human press; an independent recording needs no script section. Otherwise request missing words or draft a separate script without claiming to know an uninspected recording's transcript.

Narration changes with current or retained media impact, including retained voice or accepted cues, use `propose_change` and a typed note linking its returned `proposalId`. Pure narration writing without media impact may apply directly with a receipt. Preserve unrelated shot/section identities and settled choices. Changing text or source can make saved audio/timing incompatible; the app computes the preview. A source preference does not attach audio, start speech or mark anything accepted.

The studio uses scene confirmation. The person reviews keyframes, motion, narration and the actual video recipe before confirming. For requested video in ready scenes, record a scene-anchored `confirm_video` note choice and say: "Confirm in this chat or in Keyframes." That confirmation saves the exact script, recording and measured timing together. Listening and recording edits are optional. Do not send the person through separate per-section script, audio or timing acceptance. "Make keyframes" prepares the scene's keyframes and configured narration work; the application reports actual audio readiness and fit choices.

No narration uses the planned visual duration with no cue. Compact within-shot voiceover may be shorter than its planned shot. At 30 fps, six seconds is 180 frames. Keep reading durations labeled as estimates; never fabricate accepted cues or change visual duration merely because a voiceover is short. If audio exceeds its slot, explain the app's actual fit choices; duration/narration changes remain proposals. Legacy evidence may retain audio-led timing, but it does not add acceptance steps to the current studio.

Preserve exact audio/placement lineage for Film assembly. Changed words or pacing need compatible audio/timing established by the application; unchanged visual prompts alone prove no narration compatibility. For explicitly requested speech or transcription, follow [audio requests](audio-proposals.md): an eligible audio attachment sent with this fresh editing request can start transcription, while other audio work remains a pending suggestion for a human press. A requested `make_keyframes` run includes its needed voice lines. Their returned media/words do not attach or adopt themselves. Preserve unresolved operation identity; never resubmit blindly. Configured credentials need no extra approval or usage allowance.
