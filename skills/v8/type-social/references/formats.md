# Social formats by subtype

Each subtype is a structure. All are vertical-first, 15–20 s by default, text-free, and land with sound off.

| Subtype | Shape | Works with OpenSlate | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| `skit` | Setup → build → beat before → punch → button; one premise | Best | Dialogue needs lip sync; stage voices off-face |
| `meme` | A familiar situation pushed to its logical extreme; deadpan | Best | Must not need a caption; turn it into one voice line or a wordless reveal |
| `pov` | The camera is the viewer; characters react to "you" | Fair | Direct-to-lens reactions read well; the "POV:" label is text, so make the situation readable from frame 1 |
| `trend` | A known format with a twist | Limited | Trending sounds are licensed per platform; never depend on a copyrighted track; borrow the structure, not the audio |
| `day_in_the_life` | Five or six moments of one day, one through-line, a turn near the end | Good | Keep one character and wardrobe per time of day; 3–4 s per moment |
| `storytime` | A voice tells a short true-feeling story over evidence shots | Fair | Voice over action, not a talking head; one line per shot |
| `tips` | Hook with the result, then three tips, then the result again | Good | Number tips in the voice, not on screen; hands simple |
| `before_after` | The before state, the change, the after reveal on a cut | Best | The after image is a keyframe; keep camera and framing identical across the two states |

## Engines

- **Laugh**: `craft-comedy`. Premises from shared annoyances, status swaps, tone mismatch, scale shifts, misdirection.
- **Wow**: show the result's promise in frame 1 without the result, build, reveal on a cut. Satisfying motion (a pour, a fold, a transformation) is fragile; keep it to one simple move.
- **Teach**: one tip per shot, the strongest last; the final frame returns to the hook's result.
- **Relate**: a mini-story with one turn ("every day… until…"), a mirrored first and last image.

## What the feed rewards

- Sends per reach (a viewer DMs it to someone) and watch-through matter most for reaching new viewers; write for "I have to send this to…".
- Rewatch: a loop where the last frame flows into the first; a punch that rewards a second look.
- Original content ranks above reposts.
- Comments come from recognition: a premise that names a shared situation invites tags.

## Beat maps for 20 s

| Beat | 4 shots × 5 s | 3 shots (6 + 6 + 8 s) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook / setup | Shot 1, 0–5 s | Shot 1, 0–6 s |
| Build | Shots 2–3, 5–15 s (rule of three) | Shot 2, 6–12 s, ends on the beat before |
| Punch + button / loop | Shot 4, 15–20 s | Shot 3, 12–20 s |

## Persona and voice

- No real creators or influencers. An invented persona is a character (`craft-characters`) with fixed identity wording.
- Voice: one line at most for comedy; up to about 3 words per second for storytime or tips.
- Realistic synthetic people need the platform's AI label; remind once.

## Intake defaults

Platform IG Reels or TikTok, organic, 20 s, invented characters, family-friendly. Never ask for the joke; offer premises instead.
