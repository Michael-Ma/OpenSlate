# Social vertical format

Loaded with any type when the film is 9:16 or for a vertical-first platform. The type package sets the structure; this package sets how it reads on a phone. Read [the platform guide](references/guide.md) for safe zones and per-platform notes.

## Frame

- Keyframes, clips and the export are 9:16 (portrait 1024×1536 keyframes, 720×1280 export). Compose for portrait from the first prompt; never plan a landscape frame and hope it crops.
- Safe zone: keep faces, props and the punch out of the top 14 % and bottom 35 %, and the right edge below mid-height (platform buttons). Put the subject in the middle band.
- Close and medium frames fill the phone; a wide needs one big, simple subject. Stack subject and context vertically.
- The frame-1 subject should also sit inside the centre 3:4 crop that grids and previews show.

## Time

- Frame 1 is the hook and must read as a thumbnail: one subject, big, clear, with an odd detail or a result promised. No fade-in, title or logo opening.
- The proposition is clear by 3 s. A change every few seconds (new angle, scale or reveal) holds attention; with 3–15 s clips, put changes inside clips (a reveal, an end-state hit) rather than adding shots.
- Default length 15–20 s for organic posts, 9–20 s for ads. End on a frame that cuts back to the first (a loop) or on a button.

## Sound and text

- Design for sound on and test muted. Music carries tone; a cut to silence is the cheapest beat before a payoff. Clip sound is kept for social and comedy films.
- Text-free by default: Film has no captions or overlays. Carry a hook caption as one short voice line or a wordless image; record any caption wish in `story` as a finishing gap.
- No trending or copyrighted track is required; the person can swap music later.

## Delivery

- Realistic synthetic video should be posted with the platform's AI label (Instagram "AI info", TikTok's AI-generated label); say so once.
- No real creators, brands, logos or watermarks; an invented persona is a character.
- When a 16:9 master also needs a vertical cut, plan centre-weighted keyframes and note in `story` which shots need a separate vertical take.
